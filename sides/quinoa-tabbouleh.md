# Quinoa Tabbouleh

**Serves:** 2 | **Prep Time:** 15 min | **Cook Time:** 15 min (quinoa)

## Description
A Lebanese-inspired tabbouleh using quinoa instead of bulgur wheat, with abundant fresh parsley, vegetables, and a bright lemon-pomegranate dressing.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups tri-color quinoa, cooked
- 2 cups parsley, chopped
- 1 cup scallions, chopped
- 1 cup cucumber, chopped
- 1 cup tomato, chopped
- 3 lemons, zested + juiced
- 2 tablespoons pomegranate molasses
- 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tablespoon salt

## Instructions

1. Cook the quinoa according to package directions and let cool completely.

2. Finely chop the parsley, scallions, cucumber, and tomato.

3. In a large bowl, combine the cooked quinoa with all the chopped vegetables.

4. In a small bowl, whisk together the lemon zest, lemon juice, pomegranate molasses, olive oil, and salt.

5. Pour the dressing over the quinoa mixture and toss well to combine.

6. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving to allow flavors to meld.

7. Serve chilled or at room temperature.

## Notes

- Use cold quinoa for best texture
- Pomegranate molasses adds authentic Middle Eastern flavor
- Can be made a day ahead - flavors improve with time
- Fresh parsley is essential - don't use dried
- This is naturally gluten-free
