# Roasted Little Potatoes

**Serves:** 2 | **Prep Time:** 10 min | **Cook Time:** 30 min

## Description
Perfectly crispy roasted potatoes with golden edges and fluffy interiors. Simple seasoning lets the natural potato flavor shine through.

## Ingredients

- 8-10 Little Potatoes (about 12-14 oz total)
- 1½ tablespoons olive oil
- ½ teaspoon garlic powder (or onion powder, or a mix)
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon black pepper

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 425°F.

2. Halve the potatoes (or quarter if they're larger). Toss in a bowl with olive oil, garlic/onion powder, salt, and pepper until evenly coated.

3. Spread on a baking sheet in a single layer, cut side down.

4. Roast for 25-30 minutes, flipping halfway through, until golden brown and crispy on the edges. They should be fork-tender inside.

## Notes

- Cut side down is crucial for maximum crispiness
- Don't overcrowd the pan - potatoes need space to crisp up
- Variations: Add rosemary, paprika, or parmesan in the last 5 minutes
- Perfect alongside any protein
