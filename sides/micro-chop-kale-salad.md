# Micro Chop Kale Salad

**Serves:** 2 | **Prep Time:** 15 min | **Cook Time:** 5 min

## Description
A vibrant kale salad with finely chopped greens, toasted pine nuts and panko, creamy avocado, and a honey-dijon dressing. Massaging the kale softens it into a tender, non-tough base.

## Ingredients

### Salad
- 1 bunch curly kale, stems removed and very finely chopped
- 1 teaspoon avocado oil
- 1 avocado, chopped
- 1 jalapeño, thinly sliced, seeds removed
- 1/3 cup pine nuts
- 1/2 cup panko breadcrumbs
- 3/4 cup parmigiano cheese, finely grated
- 1 lemon, zested

### Dressing
- 1/3 cup avocado oil
- 1/4 cup honey
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1 teaspoon dijon mustard
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper

## Instructions

1. **Why?** Because the kale softens and isn't a tough ball of roughage that we all tend to think of when we think of kale!

2. First, remove stems from the kale and chop it very finely.

3. Transfer the chopped kale to a large bowl and add the avocado oil.

4. Use your hands to massage the kale for about 1 minute, to soften.

5. Chop the avocado and jalapenos and add to the bowl, along with the grated cheese.

6. Next, in a small pan over medium-low heat, toast the pine nuts for about 3-4 minutes then add in the breadcrumbs and toast for 2 minutes more.

7. Remove from heat and transfer to the bowl with the salad.

8. Add the dressing ingredients to a small blender and blend until creamy.

9. Pour over the top of the salad.

10. Add the lemon zest.

11. Toss and enjoy!

## Notes

- The key is finely chopping the kale and massaging it with oil
- Toasting pine nuts and panko adds incredible texture
- Fresh lemon zest at the end brightens everything
- This salad actually improves after sitting 10-15 minutes
- Can be made ahead without dressing for meal prep
