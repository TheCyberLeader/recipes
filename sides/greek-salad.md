# Small Greek Salad

**Serves:** 2 | **Prep Time:** 10 min

## Description
A classic Greek salad with crisp romaine, fresh vegetables, and tangy feta cheese in a simple red wine vinegar dressing.

## Ingredients

### Salad
- 2 cups chopped romaine hearts
- 2/3 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 small organic cucumber, diced
- 1/3 cup red onion, thinly sliced
- 1 tablespoon Dodoni crumbled feta

### Dressing
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
- Pinch of dried oregano (optional)
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Combine all vegetables in a bowl.

2. Whisk together olive oil and red wine vinegar.

3. Toss salad with dressing, divide between two plates, and top with crumbled feta.

## Notes

- Use high-quality feta for best flavor
- Red wine vinegar is traditional for Greek salad
- Can add kalamata olives for extra authenticity
- Best served immediately after dressing
