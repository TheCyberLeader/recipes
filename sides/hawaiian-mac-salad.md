# Authentic Hawaiian Macaroni Salad

**Serves:** 2 | **Prep Time:** 10 min (+ 4 hrs chilling) | **Cook Time:** 10 min

## Description
Creamy, tangy Hawaiian-style macaroni salad with a mayo base, apple cider vinegar, shredded carrots and onion. Must be refrigerated for at least 4 hours to develop the signature flavor.

## Ingredients

- 1 pound macaroni
- 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
- 2 carrots, shredded
- 1/4 cup shredded onion (optional)
- 2 1/2 cups mayonnaise (Best Foods or Hellman's mayonnaise - no substitutes!)
- 1/4 cup milk
- 2 teaspoons sugar
- Kosher salt, to taste
- Ground black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Cook macaroni according to package directions. Rinse under cold water until completely cool. Drain well and place in a large bowl.

2. Sprinkle on vinegar. Add carrot and onion. Toss together until well combined. Refrigerate for 15 minutes, or until cooled.

3. In a separate, small bowl, whisk together mayo, milk, and sugar. Fold the mayo mixture into the macaroni until all the noodles are evenly coated. Add salt and pepper to taste.

4. **Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours (best if overnight).**

5. Gently stir before serving, add a little more milk if needed, no more than a tablespoon or two.

## Notes

- The 4+ hour refrigeration is CRITICAL - this is what makes it authentic
- Use Best Foods or Hellman's mayo only - other brands don't taste right
- Apple cider vinegar gives the signature tang
- The salad will seem dry at first - it needs time for flavors to meld
- Can add a bit more milk when serving if it seems too thick
- Perfect alongside plate lunch or BBQ
