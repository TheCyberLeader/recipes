# Lebanese Tabbouleh Salad

**Serves:** 4 | **Prep Time:** 20 min + 30 min resting | **Cook Time:** 0 min

## Description
A bright, fresh Lebanese salad dominated by curly parsley, mint, tomatoes, and fine bulgur wheat, dressed in lemon and olive oil. The real deal — mostly herbs, not grain.

## Ingredients

- 3 large bunches curly parsley, finely chopped (about 3 cups)
- 1/4 cup fine bulgur wheat (#1 grade)
- 2 medium tomatoes, finely diced
- 4 green onions, thinly sliced
- 1/4 cup fresh mint leaves, finely chopped
- 1/4 cup lemon juice (freshly squeezed)
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon allspice (optional, traditional)

## Instructions

1. Wash and thoroughly dry the parsley (wet parsley makes soggy tabbouleh). Pick the leaves from the stems and chop very finely — almost to a mince.

2. Finely dice the tomatoes. Let them sit in a strainer for a few minutes to drain excess juice.

3. In a large bowl, combine the parsley, tomatoes, green onions, and mint.

4. Drizzle with lemon juice and olive oil. Season with salt, pepper, and allspice. Toss well to combine.

5. Add the dry bulgur wheat and toss to combine. Let the salad rest for 15-20 minutes before serving to allow the flavors to meld and the bulgur to soften slightly from the dressing. Taste and adjust lemon, salt, or olive oil as needed. Serve chilled or at room temperature.

## Notes

- Authentic Lebanese tabbouleh is mostly parsley — the bulgur is barely there, just a texture element
- Use fine bulgur (#1 grade), not coarse — it softens from the dressing alone without needing to be soaked
- Dry the parsley very well after washing to prevent a watery salad
- Best eaten fresh the same day; it gets watery if stored too long
