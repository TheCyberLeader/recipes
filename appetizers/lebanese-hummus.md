# Lebanese Hummus

**Serves:** 6 | **Prep Time:** 15 min | **Cook Time:** 0 min

## Description
Ultra-smooth, creamy Lebanese-style hummus made from scratch with chickpeas, tahini, and lemon. Far better than anything from a store.

## Ingredients

- 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained (reserve liquid)
- 1/3 cup tahini (good quality, well-stirred)
- 3 tablespoons lemon juice (freshly squeezed)
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 3-4 tablespoons ice cold water (or reserved chickpea liquid)
- 2 tablespoons olive oil (for topping)
- Pinch of paprika (for topping)
- Fresh parsley, chopped (for topping)

## Instructions

1. Add the chickpeas, garlic, and salt to a food processor. Process for about 1 minute until finely ground, scraping down the sides as needed.

2. Add the tahini and blend for another minute until well combined and smooth.

3. Add the lemon juice and process for 30 seconds.

4. With the processor running, slowly drizzle in ice-cold water one tablespoon at a time until you reach your desired consistency — smooth, light, and creamy.

5. Taste and adjust lemon juice and salt. Process for another 30 seconds.

6. Spread into a wide bowl, creating a shallow well in the center. Drizzle generously with olive oil, sprinkle with paprika, and top with fresh parsley. Serve with warm pita or vegetables.

## Notes

- Ice-cold water is the secret to smooth, fluffy hummus — add slowly to control the consistency
- Store covered in the fridge for up to 5 days; bring to room temp before serving
