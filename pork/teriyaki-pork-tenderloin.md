# Teriyaki Pork Tenderloin with Broccoli and Noodles

**Serves:** 4 | **Prep Time:** 15 min + 1 hr marinating | **Cook Time:** 30 min

## Description
Juicy, caramelized teriyaki pork tenderloin served over lo mein noodles with garlicky stir-fried broccoli. A complete, satisfying meal with bold umami flavor.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 pounds pork tenderloin
- 8 ounces lo mein or ramen noodles
- 4 cups broccoli florets
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
- Sesame seeds and sliced green onions (for garnish)

### Teriyaki Marinade/Sauce
- 1/3 cup soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar
- 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 teaspoon fresh ginger, grated
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch + 2 tablespoons water (slurry)

## Instructions

1. Whisk together soy sauce, brown sugar, rice vinegar, sesame oil, garlic, and ginger. Reserve half the marinade for a sauce. Add the pork to the remaining marinade, cover, and refrigerate for at least 1 hour.

2. Preheat oven to 425°F.

3. Remove pork from marinade (discard used marinade). Heat vegetable oil in an oven-safe skillet over high heat. Sear the pork tenderloin on all sides, about 2 minutes per side until nicely browned.

4. Transfer the skillet to the oven and roast for 15-20 minutes until the internal temperature reaches 145°F. Remove and let rest for 5 minutes before slicing.

5. While the pork rests, cook noodles according to package directions. Drain and toss with a little sesame oil.

6. In a large wok or skillet over high heat, heat 1 tablespoon sesame oil. Stir-fry the broccoli with minced garlic for 4-5 minutes until tender-crisp and slightly charred.

7. Simmer the reserved marinade in a small saucepan over medium heat. Add the cornstarch slurry and stir until thickened, about 2 minutes.

8. Slice the pork and serve over noodles with broccoli on the side. Drizzle with teriyaki sauce and garnish with sesame seeds and green onions.

## Notes

- Don't overcook pork tenderloin — 145°F with a 5-minute rest is perfect and slightly pink is fine
- Searing before roasting creates a beautiful caramelized crust
- The cornstarch slurry thickens the sauce quickly — add gradually to control thickness
- Swap broccoli for snap peas or bok choy if preferred
