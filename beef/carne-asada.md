# Carne Asada (Skirt Steak)

**Serves:** 4 | **Prep Time:** 15 min + 2 hr marinating | **Cook Time:** 10 min

## Description
Thin, charred skirt steak marinated in citrus, garlic, and Mexican spices. Perfect for tacos, burritos, or served over rice with guacamole.

## Ingredients

- 2 pounds skirt steak
- 1/4 cup orange juice (freshly squeezed)
- 3 tablespoons lime juice (freshly squeezed)
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/4 cup cilantro, finely chopped
- 1 jalapeño, seeded and minced
- 1.5 teaspoons cumin
- 1 teaspoon chili powder
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon oregano
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon onion powder

### For Serving
- Warm corn tortillas
- Guacamole or sliced avocado
- Pico de gallo
- Lime wedges
- Fresh cilantro
- Diced white onion

## Instructions

1. Combine orange juice, lime juice, olive oil, garlic, cilantro, jalapeño, cumin, chili powder, paprika, oregano, salt, pepper, and onion powder in a bowl or zip-lock bag.

2. Add the skirt steak, turning to coat well. Marinate in the refrigerator for at least 2 hours, or up to 8 hours. Do not over-marinate or the citrus will start to "cook" the meat.

3. Remove steak from the marinade and pat dry with paper towels. Drying the surface is essential for getting a good char.

4. Heat a cast-iron skillet or grill over very high heat until smoking hot. You want maximum heat for a proper sear.

5. Cook the steak for 3-4 minutes per side for medium-rare, depending on thickness. Skirt steak is thin — don't walk away.

6. Transfer to a cutting board and rest for 5 minutes. This is important — cut too soon and you lose all the juices.

7. Slice thinly against the grain (this is critical for skirt steak — cutting with the grain makes it chewy). Serve immediately in warm tortillas with guacamole, pico de gallo, and a squeeze of lime.

## Notes

- Skirt steak HAS to be sliced against the grain — the muscle fibers run along the length, so cut perpendicular to them
- Smoking-hot heat is essential for that charred, restaurant-style crust
- Medium-rare to medium is ideal — well-done skirt steak gets tough and chewy
- Flank steak is a good substitute if you can't find skirt steak
