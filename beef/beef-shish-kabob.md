# Beef Shish Kabob

**Serves:** 4 | **Prep Time:** 20 min + 2 hr marinating | **Cook Time:** 15 min

## Description
Juicy, smoky beef skewers marinated in a blend of Middle Eastern spices, grilled to perfection with colorful vegetables.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 pounds sirloin or ribeye beef, cut into 1.5-inch cubes
- 1 large red bell pepper, cut into chunks
- 1 large green bell pepper, cut into chunks
- 1 large red onion, cut into chunks
- 1 zucchini, cut into thick rounds
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 3 tablespoons lemon juice
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon allspice
- 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional)
- Fresh parsley, chopped (for garnish)

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, whisk together olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, cumin, paprika, allspice, cinnamon, black pepper, salt, and cayenne. Add the beef cubes and toss to coat well. Cover and marinate in the refrigerator for at least 2 hours, or overnight for best flavor.

2. If using wooden skewers, soak them in water for 30 minutes to prevent burning.

3. Preheat grill or grill pan to medium-high heat.

4. Thread the marinated beef onto skewers, alternating with bell peppers, onion, and zucchini.

5. Grill skewers for 10-15 minutes, turning every 3-4 minutes, until beef is charred on the outside and cooked to your preferred doneness (medium is around 135°F).

6. Rest for 5 minutes, garnish with fresh parsley, and serve with rice, pita bread, or a fresh salad.

## Notes

- Sirloin or ribeye work best — avoid lean cuts that can dry out on the grill
- Don't skip the marinating time — it tenderizes the beef and builds flavor
- Cut veggies slightly larger than the beef so everything finishes at the same time
- Serve with garlic sauce (toum) or tzatziki on the side
