# Assyrian Kebab

**Serves:** 2 | **Prep Time:** 20 min | **Cook Time:** 15 min

## Description
Traditional Assyrian kebabs featuring spiced ground lamb with feta cheese, cilantro, and beets, served with a tangy yogurt-radish sauce.

## Ingredients

### Meat
- 1 pound (450 g) ground lamb
- 1 medium yellow onion, finely chopped
- 3 1/2 ounces (100 g) brined white cheese such as feta
- 1 cup (50 g) finely chopped fresh cilantro
- 2 tablespoons (10 g) grated beet
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 teaspoon ground sumac
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon table salt

### Sauce
- 1 cup (120 g) radishes, grated, plus additional for topping
- 3/4 cups (415 ml) plain yogurt (preferably a tangy and slightly thin type)
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 teaspoon dried mint
- 1/2 teaspoon table salt
- Fresh mint for topping

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 440°F (225°C). If you're using wooden skewers, make sure to soak them in water for at least 20 minutes before using to prevent them from burning in the oven. If you're using metal skewers, no preparation is needed.

2. In a large bowl, mix the lamb, onions, white cheese, cilantro, beet, garlic, sumac, cumin, and salt.

3. Divide the mixture into eight equal portions.

4. Press and wrap each portion onto the skewers, doing your best to make them of equal thickness and length.

5. Place the skewers on a baking sheet and bake them in the middle of the oven for 15 minutes.

6. To make the sauce, squeeze out the excess liquid from the grated radishes and discard it. Then mix the radishes with the yogurt, garlic, dried mint, and salt.

7. Transfer the sauce to a small serving bowl and garnish with radish and fresh mint.

8. To serve, add the yogurt sauce to each piece of bread, then remove the kebabs from the skewers and add two of them on each bread piece alongside the green onion. Garnish with fresh mint and parsley.

## Notes

- Soaking wooden skewers prevents burning
- The beet adds moisture and a subtle sweetness
- Feta cheese keeps the kebabs moist
- Squeeze excess liquid from radishes or sauce will be watery
- Serve immediately for best texture
