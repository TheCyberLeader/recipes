# Double Beef Chili

**Serves:** 2 | **Prep Time:** 15 min | **Cook Time:** 1 hr 15 min

## Description
A hearty, meaty chili featuring both stew meat and ground beef, loaded with beans and simmered in a rich, spiced tomato sauce.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb stew meat
- 1 lb beef, ground
- 1 onion, large, diced
- 6 cloves garlic, diced
- 2 jalapeño, diced
- 28 oz tomato sauce
- 28 oz diced tomatoes
- 2 15 oz cans of chick peas
- 2 15 oz cans of sweet corn
- 1 15 oz can red kidney beans
- 1 15 oz can black beans
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 2 tsp salt
- 12 ounces beef stock

## Instructions

1. In a large dutch oven, brown ground beef on medium high.

2. When beef is browned, remove and set aside.

3. Add in stew meat and brown, turning to be sure it cooks evenly.

4. Remove stew meat when browned. Do not drain fat.

5. Add onion, garlic, jalapeño, to pot.

6. Cook on medium high until soft.

7. Add meat back to pot, and add spices, diced tomatoes, tomato sauce, and beans, stir well to combine.

8. Add in beef stock, stir.

9. Cover with lid and simmer on low for one hour.

10. Top with shredded cheese, green onion, sour cream, and fritos if desired.

## Notes

- Two types of beef create amazing depth of flavor
- Don't drain the fat after browning - it adds flavor
- Simmer for the full hour to tenderize the stew meat
- Freezes beautifully for up to 3 months
- Tastes even better the next day
