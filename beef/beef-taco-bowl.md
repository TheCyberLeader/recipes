# Beef Taco Bowl Meal Prep

**Serves:** 4 | **Prep Time:** 10 min | **Cook Time:** 20 min

## Description
A hearty taco bowl with seasoned ground beef, cilantro-lime rice, and all the classic fixings. Easy to batch cook and portion out for the week.

## Ingredients

### Seasoned Beef
- 1 ½ pounds lean ground beef
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 2 tablespoons chili powder
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- ½ teaspoon onion powder
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon black pepper
- ¼ cup water

### Cilantro-Lime Rice
- 1 ½ cups long-grain white rice
- 3 cups chicken broth
- ¼ cup fresh cilantro, chopped
- 2 tablespoons lime juice
- 1 tablespoon butter

### Toppings
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup corn kernels
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 avocado, diced
- ½ cup shredded cheddar cheese
- ¼ cup sour cream
- Lime wedges for serving
- Hot sauce (optional)

## Instructions

1. Cook rice in chicken broth according to package directions. Once done, fluff and stir in cilantro, lime juice, and butter.

2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add ground beef and break apart with a spatula.

3. Cook beef for 5-6 minutes until browned. Drain excess fat if needed.

4. Add chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, smoked paprika, onion powder, salt, and pepper. Stir to coat.

5. Add water and simmer for 3-4 minutes until the seasoning forms a light sauce.

6. Build bowls: layer rice, seasoned beef, black beans, corn, tomatoes, avocado, cheese, and sour cream.

7. Serve with lime wedges and hot sauce.

## Notes

- Use 90/10 lean ground beef for less grease
- For meal prep, keep avocado and sour cream separate until serving
- Swap ground beef for ground turkey to lighten it up
- The seasoned meat freezes well for up to 3 months
- Add pickled jalapeños or red onion for extra flavor
