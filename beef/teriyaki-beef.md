# Teriyaki Beef

**Serves:** 4 | **Prep Time:** 10 min (+ 30 min marinating) | **Cook Time:** 10 min

## Description
Tender sliced beef coated in a sticky homemade teriyaki glaze, served over steamed rice. Quick, flavorful, and far better than takeout.

## Ingredients

### Beef
- 1 ½ pounds flank steak or sirloin, thinly sliced against the grain
- 1 tablespoon neutral oil (canola or vegetable)
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon fresh ginger, grated

### Teriyaki Sauce
- ½ cup soy sauce
- ¼ cup mirin
- 3 tablespoons brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch mixed with 2 tablespoons cold water

### For Serving
- 3 cups steamed white or brown rice
- 2 green onions, thinly sliced
- 1 tablespoon sesame seeds
- Steamed broccoli (optional)

## Instructions

1. Whisk together soy sauce, mirin, brown sugar, and rice vinegar in a bowl.

2. Pour half the sauce over the sliced beef and marinate for at least 30 minutes.

3. Heat neutral oil and sesame oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat.

4. Remove beef from marinade and pat slightly dry. Cook in a single layer in batches, searing 2-3 minutes per side. Don't overcrowd the pan.

5. Remove beef and set aside. Add garlic and ginger to the pan, cook 30 seconds.

6. Pour in the remaining teriyaki sauce. Bring to a simmer.

7. Stir in the cornstarch slurry and cook 1-2 minutes until the sauce thickens.

8. Return beef to the pan and toss to coat in the glaze.

9. Serve over steamed rice, topped with green onions and sesame seeds.

## Notes

- Freeze the steak for 15-20 minutes before slicing for thinner, more even cuts
- Slicing against the grain keeps the beef tender
- Don't skip the cornstarch slurry — it gives the sauce its glossy, sticky texture
- Leftovers reheat well; add a splash of water when rewarming to loosen the sauce
