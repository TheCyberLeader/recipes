# Homemade Smash Burger

**Serves:** 2 | **Prep Time:** 10 min | **Cook Time:** 10 min

## Description
Classic smash burgers with crispy edges, melted cheese, and caramelized onions. The key is pressing the meat thin for maximum crust development.

## Ingredients

- 2 Brioche buns
- 8 ounces extra lean ground beef
- 2 Velveeta slices
- Pickles
- Salt (to taste)
- Pepper (to taste)
- 1/2 medium white onion, diced
- 1/2 teaspoon avocado oil

## Instructions

1. Create 4 even beef balls from the ground beef.

2. Lay a square of parchment paper and put a ball on it, then put another square on the top and smash each ball with a pot as thin as you can. Should be about 5/8 inch thick patty.

3. Sauté onions in a separate pan with 1/2 teaspoon of avocado oil until slightly golden.

4. Get a non-stick pan decently hot and slap the meat down, peeling the paper off. Then press them down a bit with the spatula all around. Cook for 3 minutes on one side and 2 minutes after flipping.

5. When they're browned to your liking, spoon some of the sautéed onions on and add a slice of cheese on one patty. Put another patty on top and add the remaining portion of onion, add the second slice of cheese. Cover with the lid to create steam to melt the cheese.

6. Have TOASTED (no debate on this) buns on the side. Let the patties rest on paper towel for 1-2 minutes to soak up the excess fat (this prevents a soggy bun!).

7. Put the patties on the buns, cover the buns and enjoy!

## Notes

- The parchment paper trick makes smashing easier and prevents sticking
- Resting on paper towels is KEY for avoiding soggy buns
- Toasted buns are non-negotiable for best texture
- Double-stacked patties create the perfect meat-to-bun ratio
