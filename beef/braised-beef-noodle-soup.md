# Braised Beef Noodle Soup with Baby Bok Choy

**Serves:** 10 | **Prep Time:** 30 min | **Cook Time:** 45 min

## Description
A rich and aromatic Chinese-style braised beef noodle soup with tender chuck roast, warming spices, and baby bok choy. Made easy in a pressure cooker or simmered on the stovetop.

## Ingredients

### Beef
- 4 ⅔ pounds chuck roast beef, cut into 1.5-inch cubes
- 10 servings noodles of your choice
- Salt and black pepper to season

### Aromatics & Seasonings
- 1 ⅓ onions, sliced
- 8 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 ⅔ tomatoes, diced
- 1 ⅓ tablespoons tomato paste
- ⅔ cup Shao Xing wine
- 4 tablespoons doubanjiang (spicy bean paste)
- 2 ⅔ tablespoons fish sauce
- 4 tablespoons soy sauce
- ⅓ cup dark soy sauce
- 1 ⅓ tablespoons rock sugar or white sugar
- 4 bay leaves
- 5 dried red chilis (optional)
- 1 ⅓ Chinese spice braising packets (or make your own, see below)
- 8 cups water

### Homemade Spice Bag (if not using braising packet)
- 2 ⅔ star anise
- 1 ⅓ cinnamon sticks
- 1 ⅓ tablespoons Sichuan peppercorn
- ⅓ teaspoon five-spice powder
- 1 ⅓ teaspoons coriander seeds
- ⅔ tablespoon cumin seeds
- 1 ⅓ tablespoons fennel seeds
- Cheesecloth or spice pouch

### Garnish
- 16 baby bok choy, halved
- Cilantro
- Green onions
- Chili oil

## Instructions

1. Slice onions and tomatoes, and mince garlic cloves.

2. Set the pressure cooker to saute mode and preheat on high, or turn your pan to medium-high heat.

3. While the pot is heating up, season the beef cubes on all sides with salt and pepper.

4. Add oil into the pressure cooker and add about a third of the beef. Be careful not to crowd the pot. Sear 2 sides of the beef for 2-3 minutes each.

5. Remove beef once browned and repeat batches until all the beef has browned. Add additional oil if needed.

6. If making your own spice bag, assemble it now while the beef is browning.

7. Once all the beef is done browning and removed, add the diced onions, tomato, and garlic into the pot. Saute for about 2 minutes, then add the Shao Xing wine to deglaze the pot. Scrape all browned bits from the bottom.

8. Return the browned beef to the pot and add all remaining seasonings: tomato paste, doubanjiang, fish sauce, soy sauce, dark soy sauce, sugar, bay leaves, spice packet, and dried chilis. Add the water.

9. Set the pressure cooker to cook on high pressure for 30 minutes, or simmer on medium to medium-low for 1 to 1.5 hours.

10. While the stew is cooking, prepare the garnishes and wash the baby bok choy.

11. Cook noodles according to package instructions. Remove from pot, then place bok choy in the same water to cook for 2 minutes.

12. Let the pressure cooker naturally depressurize for 10 minutes after the cooking cycle has completed, then carefully release pressure.

13. Ladle soup and beef over noodles, top with bok choy, cilantro, green onions, and chili oil.

## Notes

- Chuck roast is ideal for braising — it becomes very tender under pressure
- Don't skip browning the beef in batches — this builds deep flavor
- The Chinese spice braising packet is available at most Asian grocery stores
- Leftovers taste even better the next day as the flavors develop
- The broth freezes well for up to 3 months
