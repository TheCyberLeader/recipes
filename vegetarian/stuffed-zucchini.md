# Deconstructed Stuffed Zucchini with Brown Butter Pine Nuts

**Serves:** 2 | **Prep Time:** 15 min | **Cook Time:** 30 min

## Description
All the flavors of stuffed zucchini without the tedious hollowing! Zucchini boats filled with spiced ground lamb, marinara sauce, and topped with golden brown butter pine nuts.

## Ingredients

- 1 yellow onion
- Butter
- 6 zucchini
- 32 ounces marinara sauce
- Chicken stock
- Salt
- 3 tablespoons baharat
- 1 pound ground lamb 85/15
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Aluminum foil
- 1/2 cup pine nuts
- Parsley
- Vermicelli rice

## Instructions

1. Chop the onion and brown it in some butter.

2. Cut the zucchinis in half and scoop out the flesh, keeping it in a bowl separately.

3. Mash the zucchini flesh and add marinara sauce, chicken stock, salt, and baharat, then mix together.

4. Pour the sauce into the bottom of a baking dish.

5. Place the hollowed out zucchinis into the pan and fork them to make them tender.

6. Add lamb to the pan with the onions and cook with salt and baharat for about five to six minutes.

7. Olive oil the zucchinis and scoop the lamb mixture into each zucchini boat.

8. Cover the pan with aluminum foil and bake in the oven for about 25 minutes, removing the foil for the last five minutes.

9. In a separate pan, heat butter and add pine nuts, mixing until they are golden brown.

## Notes

- Baharat is a Middle Eastern spice blend - essential for authentic flavor
- Ground lamb 85/15 ratio provides the right amount of fat
- Forking the zucchini helps them cook evenly
- Brown butter pine nuts add incredible nutty richness
- Serve over vermicelli rice
