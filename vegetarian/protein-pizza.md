# Protein Pizza

**Serves:** 1 | **Prep Time:** 5 min | **Cook Time:** 9 min

## Description
A high-protein air fryer pizza with a crispy Greek yogurt crust. Only 630 calories for the entire pizza with 61g of protein.

## Ingredients

### Dough
- 90 g self-rising flour
- 150 g plain nonfat Greek yogurt
- 30 g PEScience Select multi-purpose protein powder
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon garlic powder

### Toppings
- 60 g (¼ cup) tomato sauce
- 56 g light shredded mozzarella cheese
- Italian seasoning

## Instructions

1. Add all the dry ingredients (flour, protein, and seasoning) to a large bowl and mix. Add the Greek yogurt to the bowl and mix until a dough forms.

2. Generously flour a clean surface and add your dough. Add more flour over the top of the dough and use your hands to press the dough out into a pizza shape, leaving the crust slightly thicker. You can use your air fryer basket as reference for how large to make the pizza.

3. Preheat your air fryer at 400°F for a few minutes, then spray with cooking spray and add your formed pizza dough. Poke holes in the center of the dough with a fork to prevent it from bubbling up.

4. Spray the dough with cooking spray, then season the outer crust with garlic powder (optional). Air fry at 400°F for 5 minutes, then carefully flip the pizza dough and air fry for an additional 2 minutes.

5. Flip the dough back in the air fryer, then add your sauce and cheese.

6. Air fry for an additional 2 minutes, or until the cheese is melted and browned to your liking.

7. Top with Italian seasoning, cut into 6 slices, and enjoy.

## Notes

- The air fryer creates the best result for a crispy crust
- For oven method: heat pizza in a hot skillet for 2-3 minutes to crisp the bottom, then bake at 500°F for about 8 minutes. Add toppings, then broil for 2 minutes to melt cheese
- For a more well-done crust, leave it in the air fryer longer before adding toppings
- Feel free to add your own toppings — peppers, onions, or turkey pepperoni work great
