# Shrimp Lemon Orecchiette

**Serves:** 2 | **Prep Time:** 10 min | **Cook Time:** 15 min

## Description
A bright and fresh pasta dish with perfectly seared shrimp, tender arugula, lemon, and orecchiette pasta. Simple yet elegant.

## Ingredients

- 150 g orecchiette pasta
- 300 g shrimp (peeled & deveined, medium or large)
- 3 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 1/4 lemon (zest + juice)
- 2 cups baby arugula (roughly two large handfuls)
- 2-3 tablespoons olive oil (good quality)
- 1 pinch red pepper flakes (optional, for a little heat)
- Salt & freshly ground black pepper
- Grated Parmesan (optional, for serving)

### Prep Work
Zest the lemon, then cut it in half for juicing later

## Instructions

### Boil the pasta

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.

2. Cook orecchiette until just al dente (they'll finish in the sauce).

3. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water, then drain.

4. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water, then drain.

### Sear the shrimp

5. Heat 1–2 tablespoons olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.

6. Season shrimp lightly with salt and pepper.

7. Cook shrimp for 1–2 minutes per side until just pink. Remove and set aside.

### Build the base

9. Lower heat to medium. Add another drizzle of olive oil.

10. Add sliced garlic and red pepper flakes. Sauté for about 30 seconds — just until fragrant (don't burn the garlic).

### Add brightness

12. Pour in the lemon juice and zest, scraping any bits from the pan.

13. Add a splash of reserved pasta water (start with ¼ cup) to loosen things up.

14. Toss it all together.

15. Add drained pasta into the skillet. Stir until the pasta soaks up the sauce.

16. Return shrimp to the pan. Toss again.

17. Add arugula right at the end — it should wilt gently in the heat of the pasta.

### Finish strong

19. Plate it up and grate Parmesan on top (optional but amazing).

## Notes

- Don't over-soak the bread for French toast - just a few seconds per side
- Wait for butter bubbles to subside before adding bread
- The sugar in the mixture helps create nice caramelization
- Challah or brioche are best, but any thick bread works
