# Shrimp Fried Rice

**Serves:** 2 | **Prep Time:** 10 min | **Cook Time:** 15 min

## Description
Classic shrimp fried rice with day-old rice, scrambled eggs, mixed vegetables, and a perfect balance of soy, oyster, and sesame sauces.

## Ingredients

- 4 1/2 cups cooked rice (day-old and cold works best)
- 1 1/2 lbs shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 4-5 eggs, beaten
- 1 1/2 cups mixed vegetables (peas, carrots, corn)
- 4-5 cloves garlic, minced
- 4-5 green onions, sliced (whites and greens separated)
- 4 1/2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 1/2 tablespoons oyster sauce (or substitute)
- 1 1/2 teaspoons sesame oil
- 4-5 tablespoons vegetable oil (divided)
- Salt and white pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. **Cook the shrimp**: Heat 1½ tablespoons oil in a large wok or skillet over high heat. Season shrimp with salt and pepper, cook 2-3 minutes until pink. Remove and set aside.

2. **Scramble the eggs**: Add 1½ tablespoons oil, pour in beaten eggs, scramble quickly, then remove.

3. **Stir-fry vegetables**: Add remaining 1½-2 tablespoons oil. Cook garlic and white parts of green onions for 30 seconds. Add mixed vegetables, stir-fry 2-3 minutes.

4. **Add rice**: Add cold rice, break up any clumps. Stir-fry 3-4 minutes until heated through and slightly crispy.

5. **Season**: Add soy sauce, oyster sauce, and sesame oil. Toss well.

6. **Combine**: Return shrimp and eggs to the wok. Toss everything together for 1-2 minutes.

7. **Finish**: Top with green onion tops. Serve hot!

## Notes

- **Day-old cold rice is KEY** - fresh rice will be mushy
- Cook rice a day ahead and refrigerate
- High heat is essential for that "wok hei" flavor
- Don't overcook shrimp or they'll be rubbery
- Oyster sauce adds umami depth
- Great for using up leftover rice
