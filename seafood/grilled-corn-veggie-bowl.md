# Grilled Corn & Veggie Bowl with Shrimp & Avocado Crema over Rice

**Serves:** 2 | **Prep Time:** 20 min | **Cook Time:** 30 min

## Description
A colorful, healthy bowl with grilled corn, sautéed shrimp, fresh vegetables, and creamy avocado crema over rice. Perfect for meal prep!

## Ingredients

- 2 cups uncooked rice
- 1.5-2 lbs shrimp (about 40-50 medium shrimp)
- 2 ears of corn
- 8 carrots
- 6 mini cucumbers
- 2 vine tomatoes
- 2 avocados
- 1 head butter lettuce
- Oil, salt, pepper, garlic powder, paprika or chili flakes

## Instructions

1. **Cook the rice**: Prepare 2 cups uncooked rice according to package directions (makes about 6 cups cooked).

2. **Grill the corn**: Roast at 425°F for 20-25 minutes until charred, or grill. Cut kernels off the cobs.

3. **Prep vegetables**: Dice all 6 mini cucumbers and 2 tomatoes. Julienne, dice, or shave the 8 carrots. Chop the butter lettuce into bite-sized pieces.

4. **Cook the shrimp**: Season with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and chili flakes/paprika. Sauté in oil over medium-high heat for 2-3 minutes per side until pink and cooked through.

5. **Make avocado crema**: Blend 1 avocado with 3-4 tablespoons water (or lime juice), salt, and seasonings until smooth.

6. **Assemble 4 bowls**: Start with rice as the base. Top with corn, carrots, cucumbers, tomatoes, and chopped lettuce. Add shrimp and diced second avocado. Drizzle with avocado crema.

7. **Season and serve**: Add final touches like salt, pepper, lime juice, or olive oil.

## Notes

- Grilling the corn adds amazing smoky flavor
- The avocado crema can be made ahead and stored in fridge
- Perfect for meal prep - keeps well for 3-4 days
- Can substitute chicken for shrimp
- Makes 4 bowls total for multiple meals
