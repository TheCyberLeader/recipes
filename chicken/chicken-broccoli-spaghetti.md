# Chicken Broccoli and Garlic Spaghetti

**Serves:** 4 | **Prep Time:** 15 min | **Cook Time:** 25 min

## Description
Tender chicken and broccoli tossed with spaghetti in a light garlic-olive oil sauce with Parmesan. Simple, satisfying, and packed with protein.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 pounds boneless skinless chicken breast, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 12 ounces spaghetti
- 4 cups broccoli florets
- 6 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 1/2 cup pasta cooking water (reserved)
- 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese, grated
- 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon Italian seasoning
- 2 tablespoons butter
- Lemon zest from 1 lemon (optional)

## Instructions

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook spaghetti according to package directions until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup of pasta water before draining.

2. Season chicken pieces with salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning.

3. Heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until golden and cooked through. Remove from pan and set aside.

4. In the same skillet, add remaining 2 tablespoons olive oil over medium heat. Add the sliced garlic and red pepper flakes. Cook for 1-2 minutes until fragrant and just golden — don't let it burn.

5. Add the broccoli florets and 1/4 cup of the reserved pasta water. Cover and steam for 3-4 minutes until broccoli is bright green and tender-crisp.

6. Add the drained spaghetti, cooked chicken, and butter to the skillet. Toss everything together, adding pasta water as needed to create a light sauce that coats the pasta.

7. Remove from heat and stir in Parmesan and lemon zest. Taste and adjust salt and pepper. Serve immediately with extra Parmesan on the side.

## Notes

- Save that pasta water — the starchy liquid is what helps create the sauce
- Slice the garlic thin rather than mincing so it cooks evenly and doesn't burn
- Don't overcook the broccoli — bright green and tender-crisp is the goal
- Aglio e olio technique: the butter, pasta water, and cheese emulsify into a silky sauce
