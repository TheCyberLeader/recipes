# Chicken Rice Platter

**Serves:** 2 | **Prep Time:** 20 min (+ 4 hrs marinating) | **Cook Time:** 45 min

## Description
Street-cart style chicken rice platter with turmeric-spiced basmati rice, perfectly marinated chicken, and an emulsified white sauce. This is the ultimate comfort food.

## Ingredients

### Chicken & Marinade
- 900 g (about 6-7 medium) boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 1/4 cup neutral oil (canola/vegetable)
- 3 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1 1/2 tablespoons white vinegar
- 3 teaspoons ground coriander
- 3 teaspoons ground cumin
- 3 teaspoons paprika (smoked or sweet)
- 3 teaspoons garlic powder
- 1 1/2 teaspoons turmeric
- 1 1/2 teaspoons onion powder
- 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon (optional but adds warmth)
- 1 tablespoon kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon black pepper

### Rice
- 1 1/2 cups basmati rice, rinsed until water runs clear
- 3 tablespoons butter
- 1 1/2 teaspoons turmeric
- 1 1/2 teaspoons ground cumin
- 3 cups chicken broth
- Salt to taste

### White Sauce (Emulsified)
- 3/4 cup mayonnaise
- 3/4 cup Greek yogurt (full-fat, plain)
- 3 tablespoons cold water
- 1 1/2 tablespoons white vinegar
- 1 1/2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 2 teaspoons sugar
- 3/4 teaspoon garlic powder (optional)
- 3/4 teaspoon onion powder
- 3/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 3/8 teaspoon black pepper

### For Serving
- 3-4 cups chopped iceberg lettuce
- 3-4 tomatoes, diced or sliced
- 6 small pita breads or flatbreads
- Optional: hot sauce

## Instructions

### Marinate

1. Combine all marinade ingredients in a bowl or bag, add chicken, toss to coat.

2. Marinate 4 hours minimum, up to overnight.

### Cook the Rice

3. Melt butter in saucepan, stir in turmeric & cumin for 30 sec.

4. Add rinsed rice, coat in spices.

5. Pour in broth, season with salt, cover & simmer ~15 min.

### Cook the Chicken

6. Heat a heavy skillet or cast iron over medium-high until hot.

7. Cook chicken 3-4 min per side until browned and cooked through (165°F / 74°C internal temp).

8. Rest 5 min, then chop into bite-sized pieces directly in the pan, tossing in the juices.

### Make the White Sauce

9. Whisk mayo & yogurt smooth, add vinegar, lemon juice, sugar, seasonings.

10. Slowly whisk in cold water until creamy and pourable. Chill.

### Assemble

11. Plate rice, top with chopped chicken, add lettuce/tomato, drizzle with white sauce, serve with pita & optional hot sauce.

## Notes

- The 4+ hour marination is crucial for authentic flavor
- Turmeric gives the rice its signature golden color
- The white sauce emulsion is what makes this special - don't skip the cold water step
- Street cart style means everything chopped and layered, not separated
- Leftovers keep well separately for 3-4 days
