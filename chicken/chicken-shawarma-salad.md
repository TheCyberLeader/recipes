# Chicken Shawarma Salad

**Serves:** 2 | **Prep Time:** 15 min | **Cook Time:** 45 min

## Description
A vibrant Middle Eastern-inspired salad featuring crispy spiced rice, tender shawarma-spiced chicken, fresh vegetables, and a creamy lemon-tahini dressing.

## Ingredients

### Crispy Rice
- 2 cups cooked rice, cooled (jasmine rice recommended)
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1 teaspoon oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder

### Chicken
- 1 pound boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into 1/2" pieces
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 teaspoons ground turmeric
- 2 teaspoons cumin
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

### Salad
- 5 persian cucumbers, thinly sliced
- 1 cup baby tomatoes, halved
- 1/2 red onion, finely sliced (1/2 cup)
- 3 dill pickles, chopped
- 1 bunch mint, chopped (1/2 cup)

### Creamy Lemon-Tahini Dressing
- 1/2 cup greek yogurt
- 1/3 cup tahini
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 1/4 cup lemon juice (1 lemon)
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- Optional: 1-2 tablespoons honey or maple syrup for sweetness

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F.

2. Add cooked and cooled rice to a baking sheet.

3. Toss with the olive oil and seasonings.

4. Bake for 30-35 minutes, tossing half way through (watch to make sure it doesn't burn).

5. While the rice is in the oven, season chicken and coat well.

6. Add olive oil to a skillet over medium heat.

7. Once the skillet is hot, add chicken.

8. Cook until fully cooked through, then remove from heat.

9. Remove the rice from the oven and set aside.

10. Next, chop/slice all of the ingredients for the salad and add to a large bowl.

11. Add the chicken and crispy rice on top.

12. Blend ingredients for the dressing until smooth & creamy.

13. Taste and adjust seasonings as needed.

14. Pour over the salad.

15. Toss and enjoy!

## Notes

- Use day-old rice for the crispiest results
- The turmeric gives the chicken its signature golden color
- Make the dressing ahead and store in the fridge for up to a week
- Adjust sweetness in dressing to your preference
- This is perfect for meal prep - keep components separate until serving
