# Mediterranean Chicken Orzo

**Serves:** 2 | **Prep Time:** 15 min | **Cook Time:** 30 min

## Description
A vibrant one-pot Mediterranean dish with tender chicken, orzo pasta, fresh asparagus, kalamata olives, and feta cheese in a lemony herb sauce.

## Ingredients

- 2 lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 2 cups orzo pasta
- 2 lbs fresh asparagus, trimmed and cut into 1-inch pieces
- 4 tablespoons olive oil, divided
- 2 medium onions, diced
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 4 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 cup kalamata olives, pitted and sliced
- 1/2 cup fresh lemon juice (about 2 lemons)
- Zest of 2 lemons
- 1/2 cup fresh parsley, chopped
- 4 tablespoons fresh oregano (or 2 tablespoons dried)
- 1/2 cup crumbled feta cheese
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Season chicken thighs with salt, pepper, and half the oregano. Heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Cook chicken in 2-3 batches until golden and cooked through, about 8-10 minutes per batch. Remove and set aside.

2. Add remaining oil and sauté onions until softened, about 5-6 minutes. Add garlic and cook 1 minute more.

3. Add orzo and stir for 1-2 minutes to lightly toast. Pour in chicken broth and bring to a boil.

4. Add asparagus pieces and reduce heat. Simmer covered for 14-16 minutes until orzo is tender and asparagus is crisp-tender, stirring occasionally.

5. Stir in cooked chicken, olives, lemon juice, lemon zest, parsley, and remaining oregano. Cook 2-3 minutes to heat through.

6. Top with crumbled feta and serve immediately.

## Notes

- Use chicken thighs for more flavor and moisture
- Don't skip toasting the orzo - it adds depth
- Fresh oregano makes a big difference
- Feta adds creaminess and tang
- This is essentially a complete meal in one pot
