# Soba Noodles with Chicken

**Serves:** 2 | **Prep Time:** 15 min | **Cook Time:** 15 min

## Description
A delicious and healthy Asian noodle dish featuring tender chicken, fresh vegetables, and buckwheat soba noodles in a savory peanut sauce.

## Ingredients

- 2 cloves garlic
- 1-inch piece ginger
- 1 carrot
- 1 bunch cilantro
- 4 ounces sugar snap peas
- 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 6 ounces soba noodles
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 1/4 cup peanut butter
- 1 tablespoon light brown sugar
- 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon sesame seeds
- 1 lime

## Instructions

1. Heat a medium pot of salted water to boiling on high. Wash and dry the fresh produce. Peel and finely chop the garlic and ginger. Peel and cut the carrot into thin matchsticks. Roughly chop the cilantro. Trim the ends off the snap peas. Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces.

2. When the water comes to a boil, add the soba noodles and cook about 4 to 6 minutes, or until tender, but not too soft. Drain and rinse under cool water to prevent them from sticking together. Set aside.

3. In a large pan (nonstick, if you have one), heat 2 teaspoons of oil on medium until hot. Add the chicken. Cook 4 to 6 minutes, stirring occasionally, until browned and cooked through. Transfer to a plate. Wipe out the pan.

4. In the same pan, heat 2 teaspoons of oil on medium-high until hot. Add the garlic, ginger, carrots, and snap peas. Cook 2 to 3 minutes, stirring occasionally, until slightly softened.

5. In a small bowl, combine the peanut butter, soy sauce, sesame oil, brown sugar, and 1/4 cup of water. Whisk until smooth.

6. To the pan of vegetables, add the cooked noodles, cooked chicken, and peanut sauce. Cook 2 to 3 minutes, tossing frequently, until well combined and heated through.

7. Remove from heat. Stir in half the cilantro and the juice of half the lime. Season with salt and pepper to taste.

8. Divide between plates. Garnish with the sesame seeds and remaining cilantro. Serve with the remaining lime wedges on the side. Enjoy!

## Notes

- Don't overcook soba noodles - they should be tender but not mushy
- Rinse cooked noodles to prevent sticking
- Peanut sauce can be made ahead and stored in fridge
- Add more water to sauce if it's too thick
- Great for meal prep - keeps well in fridge for 3-4 days
