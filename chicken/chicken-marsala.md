# Chicken Marsala

**Serves:** 6 | **Prep Time:** 15 min | **Cook Time:** 35 min

## Description
A classic Italian-American dish with tender chicken in a rich, savory Marsala wine and mushroom sauce.

## Ingredients
- 6 thin boneless skinless chicken breasts
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 6 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 450 grams cremini or button mushrooms, sliced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 cup dry Marsala wine
- 3/4 cup chicken broth
- 1/3 cup heavy cream
- 3 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped

## Instructions

1. Place the chicken breasts between two sheets of plastic wrap and pound to about ¼-inch thickness. Even though the breasts are already thin, pounding ensures uniform thickness for even cooking and a more tender result.

2. Mix the flour, salt, black pepper, and garlic powder in a shallow dish. Dredge each chicken breast in the seasoned flour, shaking off any excess.

3. Heat the olive oil and 3 tablespoons of the butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the chicken in batches, 3–4 minutes per side, until golden brown. Transfer to a plate and set aside.

4. In the same skillet, add the remaining 3 tablespoons of butter and sauté the mushrooms over medium-high heat for 4–5 minutes until browned and tender. Add the minced garlic and cook for 1 more minute.

5. Pour in the Marsala wine and scrape up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Let it simmer for 2–3 minutes to reduce slightly.

6. Add the chicken broth and heavy cream to the skillet. Stir to combine and bring to a gentle simmer.

7. Return the chicken to the skillet. Simmer for 5–6 minutes until the chicken is cooked through (165°F internal temp) and the sauce has thickened. Taste and adjust seasoning. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve immediately over pasta, mashed potatoes, or rice.

## Notes
- Use a dry Marsala, not sweet — it makes a big difference. Look for "Marsala Secco" on the label. In a pinch, dry sherry works as a substitute.
- Swap the heavy cream for a splash more broth if you prefer a lighter sauce.
- Great served with egg noodles, fettuccine, creamy mashed potatoes, or crusty bread to soak up the sauce.
