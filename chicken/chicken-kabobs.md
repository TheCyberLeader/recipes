# Juicy Marinated Chicken Kabobs

**Serves:** 2 | **Prep Time:** 1 hr (marinating) | **Cook Time:** 15 min

## Description
Perfectly grilled chicken kabobs with a sweet and tangy marinade featuring grapefruit, soy sauce, and fresh ginger. The vegetables get perfectly charred while the chicken stays juicy.

## Ingredients

### Chicken Marinade
- 2 pounds boneless skinless chicken breast or chicken thighs
- 1 cup grapefruit soda or lemon lime
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 1/4 cup brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon prepared horseradish
- 1 tablespoon minced garlic
- 1 tablespoon grated ginger

### Kabobs
- 1 medium zucchini sliced into 1/4-inch coins
- 1 medium red bell pepper cut into 1-inch chunks
- 1 medium green bell pepper cut into 1-inch chunks
- 1 medium red onion cut into 1-inch chunks
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon onion powder

## Instructions

1. Cut 2 pounds boneless skinless chicken breast into 1 1/2 inch cubes. Place chicken breast pieces in a gallon-size resealable plastic bag. Pour in 1 cup grapefruit soda, 1/4 cup soy sauce, 1 tablespoon sesame oil, 1/4 cup brown sugar, 1 tablespoon prepared horseradish, 1 tablespoon minced garlic, and 1 tablespoon grated ginger. Gently massage the bag to combine all of the ingredients and make sure all of the chicken pieces are coated.

2. Refrigerate and let marinate at least 1 hour and up to 4 hours.

3. Soak wooden skewers in water for at least 30 minutes before use or use metal skewers.

4. Prepared the vegetables by slicing 1 medium zucchini into 1/4-inch coins, and cut 1 medium red bell pepper, 1 medium green bell pepper, and 1 medium red onion into 1-inch chunks. Transfer to a large mixing bowl and toss with 1 tablespoon vegetable oil, 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder, and 1/2 teaspoon onion powder to coat.

5. Preheat a grill to high heat (500°F). Remove the chicken from the fridge and thread onto skewers, alternating with pieces of the vegetables as desired.

6. Grill your chicken on a well-oiled grate, about ten minutes, until the chicken is cooked to an internal temperature of 165 degrees F, rotating every few minutes so that all sides receive grill marks. Serve hot.

## Notes

- Marinating for at least 1 hour is crucial for flavor - up to 4 hours is even better
- Don't skip soaking wooden skewers - prevents burning
- The grapefruit soda adds a unique sweet-tart flavor
- Cut vegetables and chicken to similar sizes for even cooking
- Internal temp of 165°F ensures perfectly cooked chicken
