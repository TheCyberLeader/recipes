# Chicken Parmesan

**Serves:** 4 | **Prep Time:** 20 min | **Cook Time:** 30 min

## Description
Crispy breaded chicken cutlets topped with marinara sauce and melted mozzarella and Parmesan cheese. A classic Italian-American comfort dish.

## Ingredients

- 4 boneless skinless chicken breasts, pounded thin
- 2 large eggs, beaten
- 1 cup Italian breadcrumbs
- 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese, grated (divided)
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 1.5 cups marinara sauce
- 1.5 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
- Fresh basil leaves (for garnish)

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Line a baking sheet with a wire rack.

2. Set up a breading station: beaten eggs in one shallow dish, and breadcrumbs mixed with 1/4 cup Parmesan, garlic powder, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper in another.

3. Pound the chicken breasts to an even 1/4-inch thickness. Dip each breast in egg, then press firmly into the breadcrumb mixture to adhere on both sides.

4. Heat olive oil in a large oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the breaded chicken for 3-4 minutes per side until golden brown and crispy. Work in batches to avoid crowding.

5. Transfer seared chicken to the prepared baking rack. Spoon marinara sauce over each cutlet, then top with shredded mozzarella and remaining Parmesan.

6. Bake at 400°F for 15-18 minutes, until the cheese is melted and bubbling and the chicken reaches 165°F internal temperature.

7. Garnish with fresh basil and serve immediately over pasta or with a side salad.

## Notes

- Pounding the chicken to 1/4-inch thickness is key — even thickness means even cooking and a more tender cutlet
- Don't skip searing before baking; it's what gives you that crispy crust
- Use a wire rack on the baking sheet so the bottom stays crispy and doesn't steam
- For extra crunch, use panko breadcrumbs instead of Italian breadcrumbs
