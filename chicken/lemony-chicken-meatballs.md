# Lemony Chicken Meatballs

**Serves:** 2 | **Prep Time:** 15 min | **Cook Time:** 30 min

## Description
Tender chicken ricotta meatballs with a bright lemon flavor, served over garlic spinach orzo. This dish combines Italian comfort with fresh Mediterranean flavors.

## Ingredients

### For the Chicken Ricotta Meatballs
- 1 pound ground chicken
- 1/2 cup ricotta cheese
- 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 1/4 cup breadcrumbs
- 1 large egg
- 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 tablespoon lemon zest
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- Salt and pepper, to taste

### For the Garlic Spinach Orzo
- 1 cup orzo pasta
- 2 cups chicken broth
- 2 cups fresh spinach, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- Lemon wedges, for garnish

## Instructions

### Prepare the Meatballs

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.

2. In a bowl, gently mix together the ground chicken, ricotta, Parmesan, breadcrumbs, egg, parsley, lemon zest, garlic powder, salt, and pepper until just combined.

3. Form the mixture into 1½-inch meatballs and place them on the prepared baking sheet.

4. Bake the meatballs for 20-25 minutes, or until golden and fully cooked.

### Make the Orzo

5. Bring the chicken broth to a boil in a medium saucepan, then add the orzo. Cook uncovered, stirring occasionally, for 8-10 minutes until just tender.

### Sauté the Spinach

6. While the orzo is cooking, heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and sauté for 1-2 minutes until fragrant.

7. Add the chopped spinach to the skillet and cook for 2-3 minutes until wilted. Season with salt and pepper.

### Assemble and Serve

8. Drain any excess liquid from the orzo and mix it into the sautéed spinach and garlic until well combined and creamy.

9. Serve the garlic spinach orzo topped with the baked meatballs. Garnish with lemon wedges for an extra burst of flavor.

## Notes

- The ricotta keeps these meatballs incredibly moist and tender
- Don't overmix the meatball mixture - gentle mixing ensures tender meatballs
- Fresh lemon zest is key for the bright citrus flavor
- The spinach wilts down significantly, so don't worry if it seems like a lot
- Prep time: 15 minutes, Cook time: 30 minutes
