# Chipotle Chicken Burrito

**Serves:** 4 | **Prep Time:** 20 min | **Cook Time:** 25 min

## Description
Smoky chipotle-marinated chicken with cilantro-lime rice, black beans, pico de gallo, and sour cream wrapped in a warm flour tortilla. Better than the restaurant.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 pounds boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 4 large flour tortillas (burrito size)
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1.5 cups shredded Mexican cheese blend
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 1 cup guacamole or sliced avocado
- 1 cup pico de gallo or salsa

### Chipotle Marinade
- 2 chipotle peppers in adobo sauce, minced
- 2 tablespoons adobo sauce (from the can)
- 3 tablespoons lime juice
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon olive oil

### Cilantro-Lime Rice
- 1 cup long-grain white rice
- 2 cups chicken broth
- 2 tablespoons lime juice
- 1/4 cup cilantro, chopped
- 1/2 teaspoon salt

## Instructions

1. Mix all marinade ingredients together. Add chicken thighs and toss to coat. Marinate at least 30 minutes (or up to overnight in the fridge).

2. Cook the rice: bring chicken broth to a boil, add rice and salt, reduce heat to low, cover and simmer 18 minutes. Fluff with a fork and stir in lime juice and cilantro.

3. Heat a grill pan or skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the marinated chicken for 6-7 minutes per side until cooked through (165°F). Let rest 5 minutes, then slice or chop into pieces.

4. Warm black beans in a small saucepan with a pinch of cumin and salt.

5. Warm the tortillas in a dry skillet or microwave (wrap in a damp paper towel) until pliable.

6. Build the burritos: layer rice, beans, chicken, cheese, pico de gallo, sour cream, and guacamole down the center of each tortilla. Fold in the sides and roll up tightly.

7. Optional: return the wrapped burrito to the skillet and sear seam-side down for 1-2 minutes for a crispy exterior.

## Notes

- Chicken thighs stay juicier than breasts and hold up better to the bold marinade
- Don't overfill the burrito or it will be impossible to wrap
- Searing the wrapped burrito seals it and adds great texture
- Freeze assembled burritos (without sour cream and guac) wrapped in foil for easy meal prep
