# Chicken Cilantro Wonton Soup with Mushrooms & Bok Choy

**Serves:** 2 | **Prep Time:** 20 min | **Cook Time:** 15 min

## Description
A comforting Asian-inspired soup with delicate chicken cilantro wontons, earthy mushrooms, and tender bok choy in a fragrant ginger-garlic broth.

## Ingredients

- 8 cups chicken broth
- 2 cups water
- 3 cloves garlic, smashed
- 1-inch piece fresh ginger, sliced
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon rice wine or mirin
- 5 oz mushrooms (shiitake or cremini), sliced
- 3 small heads baby bok choy, quartered lengthwise
- 48 Bibigo mini chicken cilantro wontons (8 per person)
- 2-3 green onions, sliced
- 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
- Sesame oil for drizzling
- Salt and white pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. In a large pot, bring chicken broth, water, garlic, and ginger to a boil.

2. Add soy sauce, rice wine, and sliced mushrooms. Reduce heat and simmer for 5 minutes.

3. Add bok choy and cook for 2-3 minutes until tender.

4. Add wontons and heat through for 2-3 minutes (they're pre-cooked).

5. Remove and discard garlic and ginger pieces. Taste and adjust seasoning with salt and white pepper if needed.

6. Ladle soup into bowls and top each with green onions, fresh cilantro, and a drizzle of sesame oil.

## Notes

- Using pre-made Bibigo wontons makes this incredibly quick
- Fresh ginger is essential for authentic flavor
- Don't overcook the bok choy - it should still have some bite
- The wontons are already cooked, just need heating through
