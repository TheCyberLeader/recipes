# Chicken Taco Bowl

**Serves:** 4 | **Prep Time:** 15 min | **Cook Time:** 20 min

## Description
Seasoned chicken over cilantro-lime rice with all the taco fixings. A customizable bowl that's perfect for meal prep and weeknight dinners.

## Ingredients

### Chicken
- 1 ½ pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 tablespoon chili powder
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- ½ teaspoon onion powder
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon black pepper

### Cilantro-Lime Rice
- 1 ½ cups long-grain white rice
- 3 cups chicken broth
- ¼ cup fresh cilantro, chopped
- 2 tablespoons lime juice
- 1 tablespoon butter

### Toppings
- 1 cup black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup corn kernels
- 1 cup pico de gallo
- 1 avocado, diced
- ½ cup shredded Mexican blend cheese
- ¼ cup sour cream
- Lime wedges for serving

## Instructions

1. Season chicken thighs with chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, smoked paprika, onion powder, salt, and pepper.

2. Cook rice in chicken broth according to package directions. Once done, fluff and stir in cilantro, lime juice, and butter.

3. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook chicken 5-6 minutes per side until cooked through (165°F internal).

4. Let chicken rest for 5 minutes, then slice or chop into bite-sized pieces.

5. Warm black beans and corn in a small saucepan or microwave.

6. Build bowls: layer cilantro-lime rice, chicken, black beans, corn, pico de gallo, avocado, cheese, and sour cream.

7. Squeeze lime wedges over top and serve.

## Notes

- Swap chicken thighs for breasts if preferred — thighs stay juicier
- Rice and chicken can be prepped ahead for quick assembly
- Add a drizzle of hot sauce or chipotle mayo for extra kick
- The seasoning blend also works great on shrimp or steak
