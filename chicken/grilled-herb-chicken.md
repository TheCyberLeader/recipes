# Grilled Herb Chicken

**Serves:** 4 (24 oz chicken) | **Prep Time:** 20 min | **Cook Time:** 15 min

## Description
Perfectly seasoned chicken with a fragrant herb crust, cooked in a cast iron pan. The combination of fresh rosemary, thyme, and sage creates an aromatic, crispy exterior while keeping the chicken juicy inside.

## Ingredients

- 24 oz chicken (breasts or thighs)
- 1½ tablespoons fresh rosemary, finely chopped (or 1½ teaspoons dried)
- 1½ tablespoons fresh thyme leaves (or 1½ teaspoons dried)
- 1½ tablespoons fresh sage, finely chopped (or 1½ teaspoons dried)
- 1 tablespoon garlic powder
- 1½ teaspoons salt
- ¾ teaspoon black pepper
- 3 tablespoons olive oil

## Instructions

1. Combine all herbs and spices in a small bowl and set aside.

2. Pat the chicken dry with paper towels, then rub all over with the olive oil.

3. Coat chicken evenly with the herb mixture, pressing it into the meat. Let rest 15-30 minutes at room temperature.

4. Preheat your cast iron pan over medium-high heat for 3-4 minutes. Add a bit of oil if needed (the pan should be hot but not smoking).

5. Cook the chicken without crowding the pan - you may need to work in batches. For boneless breasts or cutlets, sear 6-7 minutes per side. For thicker pieces, sear both sides then reduce to medium heat and continue cooking until internal temp reaches 165°F.

6. Rest for 5 minutes before slicing. The herbs will create a fragrant, crispy crust!

## Notes

- Fresh herbs provide the best flavor, but dried works well too
- Don't skip the resting time before cooking - it helps the seasoning adhere
- Use a meat thermometer to ensure perfect doneness at 165°F
- Pairs beautifully with roasted potatoes and a fresh salad
